## Position and boundary

The site should help designers, owners, fabricators, and buyers specify sandblasted/frosted architectural glass: process choice, glass make-up, privacy/light, patterns, applications, quality, fabrication sequence, care, installation, and procurement. It does not provide safety-glass engineering, structural calculations, hazardous blasting instructions, or unsupported opacity/durability claims.

## Evidence gates

- Glass type, heat treatment, lamination, thickness, dimensions, holes/notches, edges, supports, impact, breakage, loads, and safety classification require current standards, traceable certificates, and competent design.
- Sandblasting may need to precede tempering depending on fabricator/process; the approved sequence must come from the responsible glass processor.
- Opacity, visible-light, privacy, colour, texture, cleanability, stain resistance, coating, and durability require representative samples and named test/product data.
- Artwork, logos, patterns, and cultural motifs require rights and approval.
- Prices, lead times, warranties, and service life require dated evidence; chipped/cracked glass requires isolation and competent assessment.
